What is Roamify?

Roamify is a collection of small, free, browser-based tools for RoamResearch users. Every tool solves one boring, repetitive task — moving your notes between RoamResearch and the rest of your writing stack.

RoamResearch stores data in its own way: tables are indented lists that start with {{[[table]]}}, highlights use ^^double carets^^ instead of Markdown's ==equals==, and supporting citations hang as indented > blockquotes under your paragraphs. Rewriting that by hand is tedious — Roamify does it in one click.

  • Roamify Tables — HTML & Markdown table code ↔ RoamResearch {{[[table]]}} lists
  • Roamify Highlights — Markdown ==highlight== ↔ RoamResearch ^^highlight^^
  • Roamify Body — strips indented > blockquotes from notes copied in Bullet, Document or Number mode

Frequently asked questions

Is Roamify really free?

Yes. All Roamify tools are 100% free, with no sign-up, no account and no usage limits. Every conversion runs locally in your own browser.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Every conversion happens entirely in your browser tab. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or tracked.

What tools does Roamify offer?

Three converters for RoamResearch users: Roamify Tables (HTML & Markdown tables ↔ RoamResearch {{[[table]]}} lists), Roamify Highlights (Markdown ==highlights== ↔ RoamResearch ^^highlights^^) and Roamify Body (strips indented > blockquotes to keep only your note's body text).

Do I need to install anything?

No. Roamify runs in any modern web browser, on desktop and mobile. Open a tool, paste your code, hit Convert, and copy the result.
